Vercy – Oversized T-Shirts Campaign

Building trust before purchase by addressing customer objections.

Client

Concept Project – Streetwear Brand

Goal

Increase messages and reduce hesitation before purchasing online.

Challenge

Customers feared wrong sizing, poor quality, and products looking different from the photos.

Strategy

Address objections first, then build trust with guarantees and risk reversal.
